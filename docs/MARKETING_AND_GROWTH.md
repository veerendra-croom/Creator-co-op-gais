## 3. Viral Loops & Referral Mechanics

### 3.1 Creator Share Links (`/u/{handle}`)
Every user profile generates an indexed web preview link (`https://creator-studio.app/u/{username}`). When creators share their portfolio cards on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or YouTube community tabs, clicking the link on Android launches the native app directly to their profile card via App Links.

### 3.2 Syndicate Co-Op Invites (`/invite/{code}`)
Workspaces generate tamper-evident team invitation links (`https://creator-studio.app/invite/{code}`). New collaborators downloading the app bypass standard onboarding and immediately enter the team's shared workspace and signing enclave.
